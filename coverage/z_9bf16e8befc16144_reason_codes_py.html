5class ReasonCode(IntEnum): 

6 """ 

7 Reason codes of MQTT 5.0 (section 2.4). A value is an int, so it can be 

8 passed where a reason code is expected and compared with ints. 

9 

10 Some values have several names in the specification, depending on the 

11 packet: 0x00 is "Success", "Normal disconnection" and "Granted QoS 0". 

12 They are aliases of one member, which is shown as SUCCESS. 

13 

14 A value which isn't in the specification, e.g. sent by a broker which 

15 breaks it, gives a member named like UNKNOWN_0x42 instead of ValueError. 

16 """
